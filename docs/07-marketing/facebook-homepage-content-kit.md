## 1. Page Bio and About

### Short Bio

### About Section

Tikpal is an all-in-one high-fidelity home audio system designed to help a room shift into focus, calm, sleep, or dedicated listening.

The prototype brings amplifier, DAC, speakers, streamer, display, and room-focused software into one quiet Hi-Fi object. It is designed around a 2560 x 720 ambient display, a calm room canvas, and direct music control when needed.

Before crowdfunding, we are sharing the product development process in public: hardware decisions, sound tuning notes, interface tests, design tradeoffs, prototype diaries, and community review.

Tikpal is for people who care about sound, materials, everyday rituals, and the feeling of a listening room.

## 2. Brand Voice Guide

### Voice Summary

Tikpal speaks like a careful product builder in a listening room: precise, warm, restrained, and transparent.

### Voice Principles

| Principle | Use | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Honest | "We are testing a warmer vocal preset this week." | "Perfect sound for everyone." |
| Technical but accessible | Explain DAC, streamer, EQ, display, and room modes in plain language. | Dense spec lists without context. |
| Calm | Use short sentences, measured claims, and quiet confidence. | Urgency, countdown pressure, or hype language. |
| Premium | Talk about integration, materials, sound, display, and long-term use. | Luxury cliches or inflated status language. |
| Build-in-public | Share decisions, tradeoffs, tests, and open questions. | Pretending every decision is already solved. |

### Claim Guardrails

- Do not make medical sleep, stress, focus, or health claims.
- Do not say "best", "perfect", "revolutionary", "game-changing", or "audiophile-grade" unless backed by published measurement and context.
- Do not promise shipping dates, pricing, certifications, final dimensions, final amplifier power, or final acoustic performance until confirmed.
- Use "prototype", "currently testing", "we are exploring", "our current build", and "target direction" when the work is not final.
- Separate measured facts from listening impressions.
- Describe Focus, Calm, and Sleep as room modes or listening rituals, not treatment outcomes.

### Preferred Language

| Topic | Preferred Wording |
| --- | --- |
| Product | all-in-one Hi-Fi home audio system, quiet Hi-Fi object, room-focused audio system |

- Perfect

## 4. Weekly Build Log Template

Use once per week. Keep it specific and calm.

### Template

```text
Build Log: Week {week_number}

This week we worked on {main_area}.

What changed:
- {change_1}
- {change_2}
- {change_3}

Why it matters:
{Plain-language explanation of the listener or product benefit.}

What we tested:
- {test_1}
- {test_2}

What is still open:
- {open_question_1}
- {open_question_2}

Next:
{Next build step.}

We are sharing these notes before crowdfunding so the product direction is visible early.
```

### Example

```text
Build Log: Week 2

This week we focused on the room canvas and source handoff states.

What changed:
- The ambient screen now stays quiet by default.
- The source picker treats AirPlay, Bluetooth, and DLNA as connection states, not ordinary playlists.
- Hi-Fi mode keeps music controls available without turning the display into a tablet-style app.

Why it matters:
Tikpal should feel like a listening object first. Controls need to be fast, but they should not dominate the room.

What we tested:
- Source switching behavior.
- Low-information display states.
- Early sound preset naming.

What is still open:
- How much metadata should stay visible on the ambient screen?
- Which skin direction feels most at home in a listening room?

Next:
We will publish a design vote on display density and continue tuning the warm preset.
```

## 7. Prototype Diary Template

Use when showing imperfect, in-progress work. This is the most important format for trust.

### Template

```text
Prototype Diary: {build_area}

The problem:
{What we were trying to solve.}

The test setup:
{Hardware, software, room, or workflow context.}

What happened:
{Result. Include failures if useful.}

What we changed:
- {change_1}
- {change_2}

What we learned:
{Plain-language lesson.}

Next prototype step:
{Specific next action.}
```

### Example

```text
Prototype Diary: source handoff

The problem:
Tikpal needs to accept local library playback, radio, Spotify Connect, AirPlay, Bluetooth, and DLNA without pretending every source behaves the same way.

The test setup:
Current software prototype running with the local audio backend and source state model.

What happened:
Music library and radio behave like direct playback sources. AirPlay, Bluetooth, and DLNA behave more like intake paths: the system has to open them, wait for a device, then confirm the connection.

What we changed:
- We made waiting states visible.
- We separated "armed" from "connected".

What we learned:
A calmer product still needs truthful status. Quiet UI should not mean vague UI.

Next prototype step:
Continue testing the handoff language on the ambient screen and player surface.
```

## 9. Road to Crowdfunding Post Series

Use these as a recurring series before the crowdfunding preview page is public.

### Series Rules

- Publish 1 to 2 per week.
- Keep each post focused on one reason or tradeoff.
- Never imply that crowdfunding is live until it is actually live.
- End with a soft CTA: follow, comment, or join preview list placeholder.

### Post 1: Why Tikpal

```text
Road to Crowdfunding: Why Tikpal

Most home audio products ask the room to adapt to the device.

Tikpal starts from the room.

The product direction is an all-in-one Hi-Fi home audio system for Focus, Calm, Sleep, and dedicated listening. The amplifier, DAC, speakers, streamer, display, and software are designed to work together instead of feeling like separate boxes.

We are still in prototype development, so this page will show the decisions before the crowdfunding page goes live.

Follow along if you want to see the build process before the launch story is polished.
```

### Post 2: Why All-In-One

```text
Road to Crowdfunding: Why all-in-one?

Separate audio components can be beautiful. They can also become complex for everyday rooms.

Tikpal is exploring a more integrated path: amplifier, DAC, speakers, streamer, display, and room-focused software designed as one product.

The tradeoff is discipline. An all-in-one system has to be honest about its limits and careful about thermal design, acoustics, software, and serviceability.

That is why we are documenting the work before crowdfunding.
```

### Post 3: The Room Canvas

```text
Road to Crowdfunding: The room canvas

Tikpal has a display, but the display is not meant to behave like a phone or tablet.

The default surface is a room canvas: low information, readable from a distance, and calm enough to leave on during listening.

Playback controls are still there. They just appear when needed.

This is one of the core product bets we will keep testing in public.
```

### Post 4: Sound First

```text
Road to Crowdfunding: Sound first

Tikpal is a visual object, but sound is the primary capability.

The current prototype work includes Flat, Warm, and Vocal tuning directions. These names are simple on purpose. They are meant to describe listening intent, not hide behind complex language.

We will share tuning notes as the prototype develops, including what is subjective, what is measured, and what is still changing.
```

### Post 5: Built for Rituals

```text
Road to Crowdfunding: Built for rituals

Focus, Calm, Sleep, and Hi-Fi are not just playlist labels.

They are room modes: different combinations of sound, display behavior, brightness, source control, and listening intent.

The goal is to make the room easier to enter, not to add more steps before music starts.
```

### Post 6: What We Will Prove

```text
Road to Crowdfunding: What we want to prove

Before asking for crowdfunding support, we want to make the product direction visible.

That means sharing:
- prototype behavior
- sound tuning notes
- design votes
- hardware and software tradeoffs
- what is tested and what is not final

The launch page can tell the story later. This page is for the work behind it.
```
